About Me

My name is Honesty Kabwe a second year student studying Computer Science at Mulungushi University in Zambia. I enjoy turning ideas into things people can actually click, watch and use. This website is my second step in ICT251 Web Technologies, where I am learning how HTML gives a page its structure and how CSS gives it personality. I like meeting new people, staying curious, and practising a little every day. My goal is to build clean, accessible websites that load quickly on any phone and help my community find useful information.
